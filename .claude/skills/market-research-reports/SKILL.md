# market-research-reports

Produce structured market and competitor research documents.

## When to use
When a business needs a market analysis, competitor landscape, or industry overview document.

## Structure
1. **Executive Summary** (1 page): key findings and recommendation
2. **Market Overview**: size, growth rate, key segments, geographic breakdown
3. **Competitive Landscape**: top 5-10 players, positioning matrix
4. **Customer Analysis**: segments, jobs-to-be-done, pain points
5. **Trends and Drivers**: technology, regulatory, behavioral trends
6. **Opportunity Assessment**: gaps, white spaces, entry points
7. **Sources and Methodology**: how data was gathered

## Data sources to cite
- Industry reports (Gartner, IDC, McKinsey)
- Public financial filings
- Job postings as proxy for investment areas
- App store reviews for sentiment
