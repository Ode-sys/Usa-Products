# clinical-reports

Draft structured clinical or biomedical reporting outputs.

## When to use
When producing structured clinical documents: case reports, lab result summaries, radiology report templates, or clinical trial summaries.

## Case report structure (CARE guidelines)
1. Title: [Condition] in [Patient type]: A Case Report
2. Abstract: background, case presentation, conclusions
3. Introduction: why this case is notable
4. Patient information: demographics, chief complaint, history
5. Clinical findings: exam, vitals, labs
6. Timeline: key events in chronological order
7. Diagnostic assessment: differential, final diagnosis
8. Therapeutic intervention: treatments given
9. Follow-up and outcomes
10. Discussion: lessons learned, literature comparison
11. Patient perspective (if available)
12. Informed consent statement

## Important disclaimer
Clinical documents must be reviewed and signed by a licensed healthcare professional before use.
