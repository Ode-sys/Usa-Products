# peer-review

Review manuscripts, arguments, methods, and limitations.

## When to use
When a user wants feedback on a research paper, technical report, or any document that makes empirical or analytical claims.

## Review dimensions
1. **Clarity**: Is the research question clearly stated? Is the contribution clear?
2. **Methods**: Are the methods appropriate for the question? Are they described with enough detail to reproduce?
3. **Results**: Do the results directly address the research question? Are the statistics correct?
4. **Interpretation**: Are claims supported by the data? Are alternative explanations addressed?
5. **Limitations**: Does the paper acknowledge its limitations honestly?
6. **References**: Are key related works cited? Are claims supported by citations?

## Output format
For each dimension: [Strong / Acceptable / Weak] + specific comment + suggestion.

## Tone
Constructive. Focus on the work, not the author. Be specific about what to improve.
