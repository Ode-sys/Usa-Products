# clinical-decision-support

Structure evidence support for clinical questions and workflows.

## When to use
When helping a clinician or researcher find, evaluate, and present evidence for a clinical question.

## PICO framework
- **P**opulation: who is the patient / patient group?
- **I**ntervention: what is being considered?
- **C**omparison: compared to what?
- **O**utcome: what matters?

## Process
1. Structure the clinical question using PICO.
2. Search: PubMed, Cochrane Library, UpToDate for evidence.
3. Grade the evidence: RCT > Cohort > Case-control > Expert opinion.
4. Summarize: what the evidence says, its quality, and any caveats.
5. Present as a structured summary, not a recommendation (AI must not make clinical decisions).

## Important disclaimer
Always include: "This is an evidence summary only. Clinical decisions must be made by a qualified healthcare professional."
